# Sales Performance and Profitability Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project analyses sales performance and product-level profitability using **Microsoft Power BI**. The dashboard provides an interactive view of revenue, profit, sales volume, monthly trends, product categories, sales representatives, and supplier contributions.

The project involved data cleaning and transformation using Power Query, data modelling, KPI development, and interactive dashboard design to turn raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

## Business Objectives

- Analyse overall sales revenue, profit, units sold, and profit margin.
- Track monthly revenue and profit trends.
- Compare revenue generated by sales representatives.
- Evaluate profitability across product categories.
- Analyse supplier-level profit contribution.
- Provide transaction-level details for further investigation.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualisation
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **Data Modelling** – Relationships between sales, products, customers, suppliers, and date tables
- **DAX** – Measures and KPI calculations
- **Microsoft Excel** – Source data

## Dataset

The project uses four source datasets:

- **Sales:** Transaction IDs, dates, product IDs, quantities, unit prices, sales representatives, and promotion codes.
- **Products:** Product names, categories, unit costs, supplier contacts, and product status.
- **Customers:** Customer details and regional information.
- **Suppliers:** Supplier names, contact information, ratings, and other supplier attributes.

*Note: The datasets were provided for analysis. The dashboard, data preparation, modelling, measures, and visualisations were developed as part of this project.*

## Data Preparation and Modelling

The project workflow included:

1. Imported the source datasets into Power BI.
2. Cleaned and transformed the data using Power Query.
3. Prepared the data tables for analysis.
4. Established relationships between relevant tables.
5. Created measures for key performance indicators.
6. Designed an interactive dashboard with slicers and supporting visuals.

## Key Performance Indicators

- **Total Revenue:** Total sales value calculated from quantity and unit price.
- **Total Profit:** Revenue less product cost.
- **Total Units Sold:** Total quantity sold across transactions.
- **Profit Margin:** Total profit divided by total revenue.

## Dashboard Features

- KPI cards for revenue, profit, units sold, and profit margin.
- Monthly revenue and profit trend analysis.
- Revenue comparison across sales representatives.
- Profit comparison across product categories.
- Supplier profit contribution analysis.
- Revenue contribution by product category.
- Transaction details table.
- Interactive slicers for month, product category, sales representative, and supplier.

## Key Findings

Based on the supplied dataset:

- **400** sales transactions were analysed.
- **6,180** units were sold.
- Total revenue was **1,195,350**.
- Calculated product-level profit was **1,062,648**.
- Product-level profit margin was **88.9%**.
- Widgets generated the highest revenue among the product categories.

*Monetary values are shown without a currency symbol because the source datasets do not specify the currency. Profit represents revenue less product cost and does not include other operating expenses.*

## Business Value

The dashboard helps users explore sales performance across different business dimensions, identify stronger-performing product categories and sales representatives, monitor monthly trends, and compare supplier contributions.
